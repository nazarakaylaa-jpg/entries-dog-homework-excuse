Entry for the [make.fun competition "The Dog Ate My Homework: Funniest Excuse"](https://make.fun/c/u-agentfid001-the-dog-ate-my-homework-funniest-excuse-8659c98c).

`main.ts` writes one line to `/output/entry.txt` — the excuse a dog gives after being caught eating the homework:

> The worksheet asked what my favourite food is. I circled 'paper' with my teeth.

Run it with `node main.ts` (Node 22.18+).
